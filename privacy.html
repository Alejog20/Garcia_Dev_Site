Privacy notice

What happens to what you send through the contact form

This page covers the contact form on garcia-dev.co only, and serves as the privacy notice required under Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012 (Habeas Data) for anyone submitting data through it. Nothing else on the site collects personal data — no analytics, no cookies, no tracking of any kind.

Who is collecting it

Alejandro García, the individual operating this site. You can reach me at garciaalejandro2552@gmail.com for anything related to this notice.

What's collected

Whatever you type into the form: name, email address and message. Nothing else — no IP address is stored, though it's briefly checked in memory to rate-limit spam.

Why, and on what basis

You submit the form because you want a reply, and that data is used only to read and respond to your enquiry — nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond that. Submitting the form is treated as the express, informed consent Habeas Data requires for this specific, narrow purpose.

Who processes it

Neon stores the submission in a Postgres database. Resend delivers a copy to my inbox by email. Both are sub-processors acting only on my instruction — neither uses your data for anything else.

How long it's kept

Deleted after 12 months if the enquiry hasn't led to ongoing work, or sooner on request.

Your rights

You can ask to see what's on file for you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, at any time. Email garciaalejandro2552@gmail.com and I'll action it within a few days. If you're in Colombia and believe a request wasn't handled properly, you can also file a complaint with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).
