Privacy notice

What this site collects, and why

This page covers two things: the contact form, and the basic visit statistics the site now keeps. It also serves as the privacy notice required under Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012, known as Habeas Data, for anyone submitting data through the form.

Who is collecting it

Alejandro García, the individual operating this site. You can reach me at garcia.dev.co@gmail.com for anything related to this notice.

Visiting the site

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to see which pages get read and where visitors are coming from. It does not use cookies and does not assign you a persistent identifier. Each visitor is counted using a value that is recalculated daily from your IP address and browser, then discarded, so nobody, including me, can trace a page view back to a specific person over time or across visits. What I can see is aggregate counts: how many people viewed a page, which country they were in, what kind of device they used, and which site sent them here. I use this to understand which parts of the site are actually useful and to fix the ones that are not. None of it is sold, shared with advertisers, or handed to a data broker.

What the contact form collects

Whatever you type into it: name, email address, and message. Nothing else. No IP address is stored from the form itself, though it is briefly checked in memory to rate limit spam before being discarded.

Why, and on what basis

You submit the form because you want a reply, and that data is used only to read and respond to your enquiry. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond that. Submitting the form is treated as the express, informed consent Habeas Data requires for this specific, narrow purpose.

Who processes it

Neon stores the submission in a Postgres database. Resend delivers a copy to my inbox by email. Vercel processes the visit statistics described above. All three act only on my instruction and do not use your data for anything else.

How long it's kept

Contact form submissions are deleted after 12 months if the enquiry has not led to ongoing work, or sooner on request. Visit statistics are aggregate from the moment they are collected and are not tied to any individual, so there is nothing to delete on a per person basis.

Your rights

You can ask to see what's on file for you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, at any time. Email garcia.dev.co@gmail.com and I will action it within a few days. If you are in Colombia and believe a request was not handled properly, you can also file a complaint with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio, known as the SIC.
